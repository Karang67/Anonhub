Help & FAQs

How do I start a new chat?

On the home page, enter a unique name for your chat room and click "Join Chat Room". Share the URL with others to invite them into the conversation.

How do I create a project?

On the home page, use the "Start a Project" section to enter a project name, then click "Create Project". A new collaboration document will be generated for you instantly.

Is my data private?

Yes. We do not collect personal information. Each user is assigned a temporary, anonymous name. Project and chat data are stored only for persistence and are never linked to your real identity.

Can I share a project link?

Absolutely. Share the project URL with anyone you’d like to collaborate with. They can join instantly and see real-time updates as you work together.
